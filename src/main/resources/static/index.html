Devotional Mission

The Pillars of Loka Kala parisad

Rooted in the timeless spiritual tradition of Mahaprabhu Sri Jagannath, our parishad unites devotees across coastal, central, northern, and western Odisha.

Harinam Sankirtan

Organizing daily Prabhat Pheri, Akhanda Harinam Mahayajna, and congregational Nagar Kirtans with traditional mridanga, khol, and kartals in every village and town.

Lok Kala & Heritage

Preserving and promoting traditional Odia devotional folk arts including Jayadev's Geet Govinda, Pala, Daskathia, classical Bhajan singing, and empowering village folk kirtaniyas.

Annadan & Gramin Seva

Serving sanctified Sri Jagannath Mahaprasad to pilgrims, sadhus, and providing emergency food relief, clothing, and blankets to underprivileged families across tribal and rural regions.

Satsang & Youth Sanskar

Conducting Gita and Bhagavata pravachans, character building camps, and youth workshops to impart eternal Vedic wisdom, moral values, and cultural pride to the younger generation.
